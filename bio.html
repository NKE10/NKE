02 — MY STORY

I'm figuring things out by making things.

I've always been interested in technology and the possibilities behind it. What started as curiosity about digital products has grown into an interest in how they are designed, built, and turned into something people can actually use.

I enjoy working with Figma and exploring visual design. Now I'm also working toward understanding the code behind those designs, from building websites to learning the foundations of software engineering.

I don't expect to know everything right away. I'd rather keep experimenting, learn from mistakes, and improve with every project.

"I don't just want to use technology. I want to understand it, build with it, and create something of my own."

What I'm working toward

My long-term goal is to become a software engineer and eventually build useful digital products. I'm especially interested in apps, business tools, logistics, and ways technology can solve real problems in Cambodia and beyond.

For now, I'm focusing on learning, trying new ideas, and turning small projects into experience.
